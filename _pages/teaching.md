## Undergraduate Research Mentoring

I enjoy introducing undergraduate students to research and helping them develop increasingly independent scientific projects.

### Zhiyu Ying
**Arizona Astrobiology Center, University of Arizona | 2026–present**

I mentor Zhiyu's research on the planetary nebulae M4-9 and NGC 6445. Her work is being developed into an honors thesis and a planned first-author manuscript.

### Matthew Gilles
**University of Arizona | 2024–2025**

I mentored Matthew's research on N₂H⁺ in more than 15 molecular clouds near the Galactic edge. His project developed into an honors thesis and a first-author manuscript currently in review at *The Astrophysical Journal*. His summer 2025 research was supported by $3,000 from my 2024 Steele Fellowship.

### Vrinda McBride
**University of Arizona | 2024–2025**

I mentored Vrinda on a literature-based comparison of gas and dust properties in oxygen-rich and carbon-rich planetary nebulae.

## Graduate Peer Mentoring

I have been extensively involved in peer mentoring within the University of Arizona Department of Chemistry and Biochemistry.

As a **Project Mentor**, I have mentored cohorts of first- and second-year graduate students as they progress toward Ph.D. candidacy. I have provided guidance on program requirements, oral examinations, fellowship applications, and navigating graduate school.

I have also provided oral-exam guidance and practice examinations for graduate students preparing for candidacy, as well as graduate-school application mentoring and mock interviews through **Científico Latino**.

## Mentoring & Teaching Development

I continue to develop my teaching and mentoring practices through formal training and professional-development programs, including:

- **MENTOR Institute Training, Level 2**, University of Arizona, 2025
- **MENTOR Institute Training, Level 1**, University of Arizona, 2024
- **Teaching Strategies that Work: Faculty Teaching Institute Workshop for Astronomy & Physics Educators**, American Astronomical Society, 2026

I am particularly interested in creating supportive learning environments in which students can develop confidence, independence, and a sense of belonging in science.
